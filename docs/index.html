Clinching scenarios, win range, and odds

Three things per seed, and only one of them is a requirement:

  • Win range — context. Your final season win total out of 28: the fewest you could end up with and still land on that seed, through the most.
  • Odds — context. Your chance of landing exactly there.
  • What guarantees it — the only must. The complete list of routes. Do any one of them and that seed is yours regardless of every other result in the league, even if all eleven other managers were working against you. Anything less may still work out; it just isn't guaranteed.

W9 means your week-9 matchup and M9 the week-9 median. "Any 3 of these" means any three from the list that follows it; "any 3 of the remaining 9" means any three of your other games. And remember you have twelve games left, not six — each week you play your opponent and you play the league median, and both count the same.

The number beside each seed is the final win total it takes — the fewest you could finish with and still land there, through the most you could have. Season totals are out of 28. Note how often the answer is 15 wins for the top seed, whether you're 14-2 or 3-13.

Side money

The season-long prizes

All twelve teams, ranked as the prize would be awarded today with the league tiebreakers applied.

Assuming no ties. Every number here assumes no tied matchups and no tied medians — scores always separate. Ties in the standings are handled in full, using the league rule: most ties, then most recent weekly records counting backwards from week 14, then points. That points tiebreak can never actually be reached, because every pair of teams meets at least once during the season and in that week their records cannot be equal.

Where the numbers come from. Each simulated week is drawn as a random ordering of all twelve teams — equal weight, nobody rated better than anybody else. Everything else — the clinching scenarios, the help needed, and the win ranges — is exact, solved against every schedule-feasible way weeks 9 through 14 could finish. Records and scores verified against Yahoo's own standings.
